# Compare in context

Mode: Operate. An ordinary extension of the approved entity profile and source drawer. Fair peer comparisons are authorized on 20 September 2026; ownership enrichment is deferred.

THESIS: Make the comparison's denominator as easy to inspect as its headline. Start from one entity, choose a year and CPV division, explain who qualifies, then show the focal value alongside the median and every member's sources.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve forest/ivory, Bricolage headings, IBM Plex body, existing dark theme, native selects, thin dividers and unambiguous text buttons. This is a reporting tool, not a league table of suspicious entities.

STORY: Open “Compară în context” from the profile → review the suggested year/category → apply criteria → inspect the full group's median and paginated members → verify any member's exact source records → freeze the complete comparison in a private investigation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Back to entity; a direct heading and explanatory sentence; a compact explicit-apply form; a prose receipt describing the peer group; two clearly labelled rows showing entity and group median. Sources and save actions sit with their relevant data. Mobile stacks the form and converts member rows into labelled blocks without hiding source actions.

FORM: Assigned extension, code-led. Functional HTML mock is authored before UI integration. The signature is the linked comparison: a concise group definition, a readable entity-versus-median plot/table, and a visible source button for every member. Bar widths are presentation only; exact source totals remain available. Never animate numbers or hide loading by collapsing the content area.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Method: same calendar year, dataset, CPV division; authorities also same heuristic type; source-row count between half and twice the focal count. Registered county is optional. Focal excluded from medians. At least five other members for interpretation. No claim of equal budgets, population, firm size, item prices, payments or corruption. Unknown authority type refuses automated cohort; small samples explain why. Full-cohort capture is limited explicitly to 499 peers plus focal; no silent sampling. Source identities and checkpoint bind every drawer and capture.
